🌺🫀🌺 Sometimes sadness gnaws in our bones like a wild animal. In the prehistoric era Igi learnt to ask sadness what it wanted from him. Igi had to follow a hard and painful way of solitude in order to gain free will and find the essence of being.

Someone brought me this book from Georgia. I told this someone my idea that I have more than one heart, that flowers blossom in my chest like hearts or that my heart flourishes through many hearts. A flower can be just a flower, but a flower can also be everything. If you know what I mean. 🌺🫀🌺
